FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CampusMate?

CampusMate is a student resource hub that brings together academic support, student clubs, career opportunities and wellbeing resources in one easy-to-use platform.

Is CampusMate free to use?

Yes. The site is completely free, and every resource it links to is a publicly available website.

How do I join a club?

Start with the Clubs filter in the resource library, then check your department notice board and college website for the current recruitment dates. Most clubs recruit in the first weeks of a semester.

Can I access CampusMate on my mobile device?

Yes. The layout is responsive, so it adapts to phones, tablets and desktops without a separate app.

Do I need an account, or is my data collected?

No account is needed and nothing is stored. There are no cookies, no analytics and no forms on this prototype.

Which technologies were used to build it?

HTML5 and CSS3 only. The filter, the review toggle, the FAQ accordion and the tour pop-up are all built with HTML and CSS — no JavaScript.
